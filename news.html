Short updates posted straight from team leads - tap any headline to read the full post.

Over the past six weeks, the Social Media Team contacted more than 40 academic offices and student unions across Greece to spread the word about big Volunteers and the BioInnovation Greece Forum 2026. Response has been strong, with several universities already offering to share the volunteer sign-up form directly with their biology, bioinformatics, and biotech student mailing lists.

Alongside outreach, the team finalized the content calendar running from now through the forum's opening on September 30th — expect a mix of speaker spotlights, behind-the-scenes prep content, and countdown posts across Instagram and TikTok.

If your department hasn't heard from us yet, reach out through the Join Us form and we'll make sure you're looped in.
